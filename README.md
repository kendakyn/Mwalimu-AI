# Mwalimu AI - The Ultimate Kenyan STEM Tutor

Mwalimu AI is a specialized educational application designed to make Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) subjects accessible, engaging, and culturally relevant for Kenyan students. By leveraging advanced AI and localized analogies, it transforms complex academic concepts into "mtaa" (neighborhood) conversations.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Dual Tutoring Modes
- **Complete Lessons**: Structured deep-dives into topics featuring **"Teacher Mwalimu"**, an animated AI avatar that speaks the lesson content using text-to-speech.
- **Quick Q&A**: Precise, bolded answers for fast inquiries with relevant background context, tailored for quick consumption.

### 2. Localized Code-Switching
Mwalimu AI understands that clarity is key. It supports:
- **English**: For technical precision.
- **Formal Swahili**: For traditional academic grounding.
- **Sheng**: Kenya's urban slang to make learning feel like a "chill session at the base."

### 3. Glacier Notes
Never miss a detail. The system automatically generates concise, formatted summaries from your lessons. 
- Save notes to your personal archive.
- Review key concepts in a "Glacier" themed minimalist UI.

### 4. Academic Personalization
The AI adapts to your level:
- **Education Level**: Secondary, University, etc.
- **Specific Level**: E.g., Form 4, Year 1.
- **Study Session Timers**: The AI respects your study plan, delivering content that fits within your allocated hours and minutes.

### 5. STEM Excellence
Specialized support for:
- Mathematics
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Biology
- Computer Science
- Aviation Technology
- Agriculture

## 🛠 Technology Stack

- **Framework**: [React 18+](https://reactjs.org/) with [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) with custom Glassmorphism ("Glacier") design recipes.
- **Animations**: [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/) for interactive UI and avatar states.
- **AI**: [Google Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/) for intelligent, context-aware tutoring.
- **Audio**: Web Speech API for real-time text-to-speech synthesis.

## 📖 How to Use

1. **Academic Setup**: Configure your profile with your name, level, and preferred subjects in the **Settings** tab.
2. **Select Mode**: Navigate to the **Tutor** page and choose between a "Complete Lesson" or "Quick Question."
3. **Interact**: Type your topic or question. If you chose a lesson, ensure your speakers are on to hear Mwalimu speak!
4. **Rate Limiting**: During lessons, you can ask follow-up questions, but stay focused—Mwalimu keeps you on track by limiting rapid-fire off-topic queries.
5. **Archive**: Click "Save Note" after a lesson to add it to your **Notes** library.
